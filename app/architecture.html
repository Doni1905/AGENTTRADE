n8n Workflow Engine

Acts as the backbone for the multi-agent system. Each node in the graphical workflow dictates strict bounded contexts, preventing infinite loops and ensuring structured LLM evaluations.

Qdrant Vector Database

Powers the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. Massive repositories of dated evidence cards are vectorized locally, enabling the agent to recall specific, relevant market context in milliseconds.

Local Ollama Models

Utilizes Qwen (2.5) local LLMs. Complete privacy is guaranteed by executing all complex reasoning, synthesis, and critic evaluations strictly on local hardware.
